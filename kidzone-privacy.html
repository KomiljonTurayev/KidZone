KidZone — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 April 2026 · Effective: 30 April 2026

For Parents and Guardians: KidZone is designed for children under 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy. We do not collect personal information from children.

1. Who We Are

KidZone ("the App") is developed by Turayev Komiljon ("we", "our", "us"), an independent developer based in Uzbekistan. Contact: komiljon_torayev@epam.com

2. Information We Do Not Collect

KidZone is built for children. We do not collect, store, or share:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses
  • Photos, videos, or voice recordings
  • Precise geolocation
  • Contacts, calendar, or other personal device data
  • Account credentials (the App requires no sign-in)

3. Information Automatically Processed

The App processes only the minimum technical data needed to function:

  • Device type and OS version — to ensure compatibility
  • Crash logs — anonymous, used solely for fixing bugs
  • Game progress — stored locally on the device only; never uploaded

4. Advertising

KidZone displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. In compliance with COPPA and Google Play Families Policy:

  • All ads are non-personalized and child-directed
  • We have enabled "Tag for child-directed treatment" in AdMob
  • No behavioral profiling of children occurs
  • Ads are filtered to be age-appropriate (no violence, gambling, or adult content)

Google's privacy practices for AdMob serving to children: support.google.com/admob/answer/6223431

5. Third-Party Services

KidZone uses only the following Families-approved services:

  • Google AdMob (child-directed mode) — privacy policy
  • Google Play Services — required for app delivery and security

We do not use Facebook SDK, analytics that track children, or any social media integration.

6. Parental Rights (COPPA)

Although we do not knowingly collect personal information from children, parents have the right to:

  • Request information about any data associated with their child's device
  • Request deletion of any such data
  • Refuse further data processing

To exercise these rights, contact komiljon_torayev@epam.com. We will respond within 14 days.

7. Data Security

All app data is stored locally on the user's device. We do not operate servers that store user data.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the new version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes will be communicated through an in-app notice.
